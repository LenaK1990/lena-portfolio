Making users’ loan and credit information visible in the loan consolidation flow

Product context

Kreddy is a B2C personal finance app that helps users understand their credit score, loans, and overall financial health – and explore loan options without upfront commitment. The consumer loan application allows users to consolidate existing loans as part of a new loan application.

UC is a Swedish provider of digital business and consumer information services, supplying credit and loan data used in financial decision-making. UC is an external data provider for Kreddy, and we considered their requirements and feedback when designing experiences that use their data.
