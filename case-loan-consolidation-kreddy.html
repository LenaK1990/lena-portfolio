Kreddy · Consumer Loan Application

Helping more users choose to consolidate their existing loans

Brought users’ existing loan and credit information into the application at the point of the consolidation decision.

External stakeholder

Platform

iOS, Android

Product context

Kreddy is a B2C personal finance app that helps users understand their credit score, loans, and overall financial health. Its consumer loan application allows users to consolidate existing loans when applying for a new loan.

Kreddy uses data from UC across the app, and we worked with UC on requirements for this experience. It’s a Swedish provider of credit and consumer data used in financial decision-making.

Problem

Kreddy already showed users their credit score and existing loans using UC data across the app. However, when users entered the loan application, this information was no longer available alongside the decision to consolidate their existing loans.

The existing loan overview and the loan consolidation decision were disconnected: users could see their loans in one part of the app, but were asked whether they wanted to consolidate them without that context in the application.

Existing experience

Business goal

Increase the number of submitted loan applications where users choose to consolidate their existing loans.

Design challenge

How might we bring relevant loan information into the application at the point of the consolidation decision, without disrupting the existing loan flow or making consolidation feel mandatory?

Constraints

The challenge was not only about making the information visible. I also had to work within existing product, data, and regulatory constraints.

  • Surface UC financial information – include both credit score and registered debts and credits.
  • Respect consolidation rules – mortgages and student loans could be shown, but could not be included in the consolidation flow.
  • Keep the information relevant regardless of intent – users should see the information whether or not they planned to consolidate.
  • Keep consolidation optional – no copy or CTAs could encourage users to consolidate their loans.
  • Work within the existing application flow – avoid fundamentally changing how users move through the application.
