Product context

Kreddy is a B2C personal finance app that helps users understand their credit score, loans, and overall financial health. Its consumer loan application allows users to consolidate existing loans when applying for a new loan.

Kreddy uses data from UC across the app, and we worked with UC on requirements for this experience. It’s a Swedish provider of credit and consumer data used in financial decision-making.

Problem

Kreddy already showed users their credit score and existing loans across the app, but this information was no longer available when they entered the loan application and decided whether to consolidate their existing loans.

Existing experience

Business goal

Increase the number of submitted loan applications where users choose to consolidate their existing loans.

Design challenge

How might we bring relevant loan information into the application at the point of the consolidation decision, without disrupting the existing loan flow or making consolidation feel mandatory?

Constraints

The challenge was not only about making the information visible. I also had to work within existing product, data, and regulatory constraints.

  • Surface UC financial information – credit score and registered debts and credits.
  • Respect consolidation rules – mortgages and student loans could be shown, but not consolidated.
  • Keep information relevant regardless of intent – visible whether or not users planned to consolidate.
  • Keep consolidation optional – no copy or CTAs encouraging consolidation.
  • Work within the existing application flow – without fundamentally changing how users move through it.

Early exploration

I explored different ways to bring the information into the first step of the application, testing how much could be added while keeping the existing structure and logic.

Two directions

Based on feedback, we moved away from changing the first-step logic and focused on solutions within its existing structure.
